Potatoes Au Gratin

https://www.daringgourmet.com/perfectly-creamy-au-gratin-potatoes/

Equipment

  • Stock Pot
  • Casserole Dish
  • Medium Mixing Bowl
  • Small Mixing Bowl
  • 2 Small Bowls

Preparation

  • Preheat oven to 350

Ingredients

Small Mixing Bowl

  • Yellow Onion - Diced

Medium Mixing Bowl

  • 2 pounds / 1000 grams Russet Potatoes - Sliced rounds 2mm thick

Small Bowl 1

  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups milk

Small Bowl 2

  • 2 cups fresh shredded cheddar
  • 1/4 cup grated fresh grated parmesean cheese
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

Individual Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 1/4 cup grated fresh grated parmesean cheese

Instructions

  1. Heat butter in stock pot over medium heat until melted.
  2. Add onion and cook for 6 minutes. Stirring occationally.
  3. Add garlic. Cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add flour. Cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Add milk and cream. Stir continually to prevent clumping.
  6. Slowly bring to a boil.
  7. Reduce heat and simmer until slightly thickened.
  8. Add cheeses and salt. Stir until melted.
  9. Add potatoes. Mix.
  10. Pour mixture into casserole dish and level.
  11. Sprinkle with remaining parmesean cheese.
  12. Bake for 1 hour.
  13. Uncover. Pairing knife should pierce potatoes with ease.
  14. Bake additional 20 minutes.
  15. Let rest for 5-10 minutes to allow potatoes to absorb soupiness. Enjoy.
